Requesting Reimbursement

All expenses must be approved before the event. Expenses not pre-approved will not be reimbursed. Refer to the sponsorship guidelines to see how to request a reimbursement.

Mass Payment Request Tool Reimbursement

Using the Mass Repayment Tool is a requirement of the program from GitHub starting Dec 3, 2024. In order to comply with the new rules, we've set up a system for Campus Experts to request reimbursement via a new process that should take less time from submitting your invoice to getting repaid than the previous process. We will be requiring Campus Experts to use this process starting April 7, 2025, but it will be available for all to use starting March 3rd, 2025.

Submitting your invoice

Your invoice will be submitted as a comment in the same issue you used to propose your event. You are able to request reimbursement as soon as you have spent the money on your event. You do not have to wait for your event to finish, however, you do need to wait until you’ve spent all the money you plan to spend and have receipts for all purchases. See Filling out the invoiceopen in new window for how to create your invoice.

Please follow this process for submitting the invoice:

  • Attach your invoice to a new comment on the same issue you proposed your event with. The comment should include:
    • The invoice as a PDF attachment to the comment.
    • A link to the Google Drive folder containing all your receipts.
    • A line tagging @Metzinaround in the issue.
  • After adding the comment, please add the MPR Reimbursement Requested label.

Pj will work through these issues with this label on them in order to process your reimbursements at least once every 2 weeks. Once he has processed your payment, he will comment on the issue letting you know he's sent it to Microsoft and remove the MPR Reimbursement Requested label. He will then apply the MPR Reimbursement Submitted label to the issue.

This is separate from your Post Event report process. A post event report is still required for all events Campus Experts receive support for.

Filling out the invoice (MPR process only)

Invoice templateopen in new window Make a copy of this and name it YourLastName_invoice_##### where ##### is the invoice number (see below for Invoice number instructions). You'll need to download the template as a PDF after filling it out in order to upload it to the event issue you created when requesting support. Create an invoice for the total amount, this invoice should include a list of all expenses and their amounts. Fill out the invoice with the following details:

**Attn: Pj Metz**  
GitHub Inc,  
88 Colin P Kelly Jr Street,  
San Francisco, CA 94107

(This does not mean that you need to physically mail your invoice in, but digital invoices must also be addressed here)

For the Invoice number, this indicates how many invoices you have sent the program and it should start with your GitHub IDopen in new window. After your GitHub Id, please add a 001 to indicate which invoice number this is for you. The next time you submit an invoice it should be 002 and so on and so forth. For example, if Pj were to create an invoice for the first time, it would be numbered 65838556001. Pj's GitHub ID is 65838556.

The Invoice should be filled out in the currency you paid for your goods and services in. Make sure to indicate what the currency you're using at the bottom of the invoice where it says Total (CURRENCY) and replace the wordCURRENCY, with the three letter abbreviation for your country's currency. For example, Total(USD) for US Dollars, or Total(INR) for Indian Rupees. Please change the Dollar sign in the final amount to reflect your currency’s symbol. To do this In Google Sheets, go to Format > Number > Custom Currency, select your country's currency, and click Apply.

The line items in the invoice should be formatted to say what the line item is for, and a total cost for that particular line item. For instance, if you ordered pizza for the event and it cost $250, the line item should say "Pizza" in the Description cell, and the total cost of the pizza in the Total Amount cell.

Onboarding yourself to the MPR (First payment only)

The process begins once the Program Manager has submitted the MPR, which will be done at least once every two weeks. For your first payment you’ll receive an email with more information and clear directions on what to do next.

You must complete the four steps provided under the My Accounts tab on the payment central.

Accordingly you must provide

  • Contact Info

    • You will be asked to provide basic information and contact information about yourself or your business.

  • Questionnaire

    • You will be provided with a basic Questionnaire to gather some information on affiliates of you or your business.

  • Tax info

    • The instructions will present you a tax form with the following questions:

      1. Is the account holder an individual or a non-individual entity? (you should answer "Individual")
      2. Is the account holder a citizen or resident of the United States?
      • Once you've answered these, a box called "Effectively Connected Income" pops up that says you should complete this form ONLY if ALL the conditions listed are correct. Unless you are a US Citizen, you are not filling out a US tax return, and thus you should NOT check the box. You should NOT fill out a Form W-8ECI.

  • Bank Profile

    • In this step, you will be required to submit the bank details associated with the account where you want to receive payouts.

Once all of the required information is submitted, your information will be verified by Microsoft. Once you see all of the steps marked as completed (refer image below), the next step is to wait!

Payouts should take less than 30 days from the date you submit the information requested by Microsoft.

OPEN COLLECTIVE

As of December 1, 2024, All reimbursements will be done through Open Collective until further notice. We will be moving to the preferred process of the Mass Payment Request tool starting March 3, and officially stop using Open Collective on April 30th, 2025.

Here are Open Collective's Docs on invoices vs. reimbursements.open in new window Make sure to read through and understand their process.

Submitting Reimbursements for GitHub Education Projects via Open Collective

Github Education Campus Experts partners with Open Source Collective (a 501c6 open source sustainability nonprofit) as its fiscal host and uses the Open Collective platform to process reimbursements for approved student projects.

Before You Begin

Review Open Source Collective’s Documentation Make sure you read through Open Source Collective’s ‘Invoices and Reimbursements’open in new window documentation to understand their process and what is required on reimbursements

Create an Open Collective Account

If you already have an Open Collective account, skip this part If you don’t have an account: Create a profileopen in new window.

Steps to Request Reimbursement

  • Head over to https://opencollective.com/github-education and follow the GitHub Education Collective.
  • After following, find the "Submit Expense" button
  • Next, select "Reimbursement" (For CE Events) from the two options.
  • Fill out the Payee information and add your bank details or your PayPal account
  • Once through, click "Next" and then fill out Expense details.
  • Upload all of the receipts as individual line items in the Expense.
  • Add the tag "ce-sponsorship" below "Tag your expense".
  • The remainder is straightforward: fill out the "Invoice details" section based on the receipts you're submitting, add notes if any are needed and you're all set to submit! It’s helpful to include a very short description of the type of event it was and how the purchases supported it.

You do not need to upload an invoice to Open Collective. The invoice is for GitHub purposes only. Please email your invoice to Pj at metzinaround@github.com and note that you are using Open Collective. The only thing required for reimbursements is to upload all receipts for your purchases.

Payment Schedule

Payments are processed twice a week on Tuesdays and Thursdays at 8:00 am Pacific Time.

If You Need Support

For issues with expenses, or if you’re having difficulty with receiving your payments, email hello@oscollective.org.

For account setup, password retrieval, or other platform-related inquiries, email support@opencollective.com
